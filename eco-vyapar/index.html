BUSINESS ETHICS BY DESIGN

Credibility is part of sustainability.

01

No greenwashing

Environmental claims should reflect actual observed practices.

02

Transparency

Sample size and methodology should be visible.

03

Consent and privacy

Retailer identities and photographs should only be published with permission.

04

Positive reinforcement

Encourage transition without public shaming.

TRANSPARENCY CONSOLE

Make every sustainability claim traceable.

01 / VERIFIED6-shop field sample

Observed locally; never presented as city-wide evidence.

02 / PRIVACYConsent-first identities

Retailer names and photos stay private until permission is available.

03 / INTEGRITYNo official-certification claim

Green Score and recognition remain educational and student-led.

04 / FUTURENetwork metrics = planned

Future dashboards stay clearly labeled until follow-up data exists.
